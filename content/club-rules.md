				<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-ab428cb elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="ab428cb" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
				<div class="elementor-widget-container">
									Only Members in “Good Standing”, their immediate family and their invited guests are
permitted to use the facilities of AVC. A Member is considered to be in good standing when current with
his or her dues payment and other financial responsibilities, has the signed Liability and Save Harmless
Agreement for the current season on file at the Club (i.e., Members Release) and his or her membership
privileges have not been suspended by the Board. Members are entirely responsible for their guests’
behavior.								</div>
				</div>
				<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-2be02c2 elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="2be02c2" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
				<div class="elementor-widget-container">
									No Member will be allowed into the fields at AVC before having signed a Member Release.
All guests must proceed immediately and directly to the Clubhouse to sign a Guest Release (Fishing
guests may proceed directly to the Fishing sign-in mailbox to sign a guest release) prior to using AVC
property and/or facilities. This includes but is not limited to: hunters, observers, guests, dog handlers and
trainers, guides, food caterers and support personnel associated with tower shoots including dog
handlers available for retrieving.								</div>
				</div>
				<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-f1f3da9 elementor-hidden-desktop elementor-hidden-tablet elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="f1f3da9" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
				<div class="elementor-widget-container">
									<p>Members, their spouse and children/legal dependents living at home,<br />age 18 through 24, may sign once for the season on the Members Release included with the dues notice.<br />Members may also sign for their other minor children and legal dependents as well on the dues notice<br />Members Release by listing their names in the appropriate section. <b>All other individuals must sign the<br />guest release each time they visit.</b> Minor guests under the age of 18 must have a Release, Save<br />Harmless and Indemnity Agreement for Minors (Minors Release) signed by a parent or legal guardian<br />each time they visit. This Minors Release is printed in the Handbook for your convenience. Each guest<br />must have a signed Release agreement for each visit date. Contact the Club Manager in advance to<br />coordinate access to the proper release.</p>								</div>
				</div>
				<div class="elementor-element elementor-element-d01248f elementor-hidden-desktop elementor-hidden-tablet elementor-widget elementor-widget-text-editor" data-id="d01248f" data-element_type="widget" data-widget_type="text-editor.default">
				<div class="elementor-widget-container">
									For safety reasons ALL Members and Guests must sign in with the Club Manager/Staff before
being permitted on AVC property to hunt, fish or any other activity. This includes Deer hunters who
must notify the Club Manager/Staff in advance of going deer hunting, i.e., phone, email or text. If
appropriate, the Manager will assign general hunting areas. The Member is responsible for reading the
bulletin board for special events, private hunts, or instructions. ALL guests are required to read,
understand and sign an AVC Release agreement BEFORE being permitted to hunt, fish or any other
activity at AVC. Any person refusing to sign this agreement will not be allowed to go into AVC fields or
fish AVC lakes.
Park all vehicles only in the designated parking areas.								</div>
