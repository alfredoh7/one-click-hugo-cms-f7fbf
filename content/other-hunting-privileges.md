									Deer hunting is permitted during the six separate seasons as defined by the State of New Jersey: Fall Bow, Permit Bow, Permit Muzzleloader, Six-day Firearm, Permit Shotgun, and Winter Bow that run approximately from mid-September through mid-February. The Shooting Committee will monitor the overall health and population of deer on club property and may further limit the harvest of Bucks and/or does. The Shooting Committee will provide as much advance notice as practical and post any additional harvest restrictions in Tailfeathers and the Clubhouse bulletin board.								</div>

									<p>Deer hunting is limited to Active Members, Provisional Members, Provisional Junior Members, Preview Members, Senior Members, Limited Members and Honorary Members and their immediate family members who are residents of the same household, i.e., their spouse, and children/legal dependents under age 25, and have signed the Members Release. Immediate family members must be at least 18 years of age to deer hunt alone. Any immediate family member under the age of 18 must be accompanied by the member in a two-person stand or in an adjacent stand (located in the same tree) or under close visual supervision by the member. If utilizing two stands, the specific location of the two stands will be established in advance of the hunt and only after discussion with and approval by the Club Manager.</p>								</div>

elevated tree stand or a Club provided ground blind. The only exception is if the Shooting Committee
approves a special group hunt to help manage the overall deer population.								</div>

A limited number of Active members who have grandfathered stand sites, may hunt from their “grandfathered stand.” A Deer Hunter who wants to hunt during any deer season should contact the Club Manager for stand availability on a day-to-day basis. Any Deer Hunter, with Club Manager approval, may provide their own portable stand and remove it on a daily basis for use only at the approved stand location (within a 50-yard radius). &#8220;Grandfathered&#8221; members, with Club Manager approval, may have a secondary stand location (within a 50-yard radius of their grandfathered stand). When a “grandfathered stand” member leaves the club or voluntarily retires his stand, that stand location reverts back to the club to be used as a future Club Stand.								</div>

									<p>Screw in <strong>climbing pegs</strong> (aka screw in tree steps) are <strong>not permitted</strong> to be used or inserted into tree sat any time for any purpose. This rule precludes their use for climbing up to or down from any tree stand as well as using such climbing pegs/screw in tree steps to hang accessories or another stand. All stands must be “portable”, i.e., a self-climbing stand, a ladder stand with access being via an integral ladder, or a strap hung stand with strapped climbing sticks that do not break the bark of the tree. AVC staff will remove all such climbing pegs/screw in tree steps on any existing trees or tree stands.</p>								</div>

				</div>

Members and their families should use discretion in the number of times anyone fishes the Club Lakes to limit the stress on the fishery and provide an opportunity to other members. <b>The maximum number of fishing visits per month for Lake #2 (the Middle Lake/Fishing Lake by the Point Field) is ten (10).</b> There is no fishing visit limit for Lake #1 (the Lower Lake by Queen Road) or Lake #3 (Upper Lake/Duck Lake by the clubhouse). 								</div>

									3. All fishing in Lake #3 must be done from the shoreline only. No boats are permitted in Lake #3. Please note that during the first or second week in July, the club receives several thousand ducklings that are housed on Lake #3, hence the reference to the “Duck Lake”. To facilitate their acclimatization, <b>neither fishing nor dogs are allowed in Lake #3 from the time of their arrival until the last scheduled duck shoot is completed.</b> Please check with the Manager to see what that date will be. The Board will monitor fishing visits and fish caught, and may revise in-season limits.								</div>

									4.
