				<div class="elementor-widget-container">
									The proper blaze orange clothing will be worn whenever in the field during hunting season. In
addition to the 200 square inch minimum blaze orange NJ state requirement, a blaze orange hunting cap
must also be worn while field hunting. AVC has the following additional blaze orange requirements:
Hunters must wear a cap made of daylight fluorescent orange and an outer garment containing at
least 200 square inches of fluorescent orange material above the waist visible from all sides, at all times,
when in the field or engaged in hunting during the bird hunting season or hunting from a tree stand
during the firearm season. This applies to all persons in a hunting party. Fox and coyote hunting are
permitted by active members only following State regulations and from tree stands only, but the Club
Manager must be notified in advance. Exceptions: This rule does not apply to waterfowl, side hill duck
shoots, crow, spring wild turkey, or bow hunters except that a bow hunter using a deer decoy must wear
blaze orange clothing, as described above, while transporting the decoy into and out of the woods and
fields								</div>

A broken gun with live shells in the chamber is not an unloaded gun.  NJ hunting regulations forbid shooting into a safety zone.  Neither firearms nor dogs are permitted in the Clubhouse.								</div>

				<div class="elementor-widget-container">
									The consumption of alcoholic beverages or use of any impairing substance before or during any
shooting activity by the person shooting or anyone involved in the shooting activity is forbidden.
Anyone who consumes alcoholic beverages or is under the influence of any impairing substance will not
be allowed into AVC fields. Alcoholic beverages are allowed ONLY after all shooting is completed. This
rule applies especially during prepaid and larger private shoots where increased numbers of guests are
likely and a lunch is often served between activities. Members are entirely responsible for their guests’
conduct. 								</div>
