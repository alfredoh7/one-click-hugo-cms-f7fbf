									The AVC Preview Member (PM) Plan allows prospective members the opportunity to experience
the club for a defined period without a full commitment to joining. This plan is open to all applicants 25
and older. The PM pays current dues, any assessments, and a $1500 premium for each year in the
program and receives Provisional Member privileges. The Preview Plan’s duration is limited to 2
seasons. If the PM opts to join the club, premium payments are applied to the age-appropriate initiation
fees. If the PM declines to go forward, all premium payments are forfeited. 								</div>

				<div class="elementor-widget-container">
									To be considered for Limited Membership, one must be an Active Member in Good Standing for the previous 10 years and have a medical condition that affects the member&#8217;s ability to use the club (with a doctor&#8217;s certification). Limited Members are allowed a total of 5 visits to the club per year for the purposes of field bird hunting. A Limited Member pays 50% of the annual dues, plus bird costs, and any special assessments that are imposed upon the membership. A Limited Member also has fishing and deer hunting privileges, except for the opening Monday of firearm buck season. No turkey hunting is allowed.  Exempted from the 5-visit limit in a hunting year would be any social event, or tower/release shoots, unless the member chooses to field bird hunt before or after such tower or release shoot. Limited Membership is granted at the discretion of the Board, and at no time shall it exceed 10 members. Any Wait List for Limited Membership will be prioritized based on years of Active Membership.								</div>

									<p>To be considered for Nonresident Membership, one must be an Active Member in Good Standing for the previous 10 years and have a primary residence out of state 150 miles or more from the club. A Nonresident Member pays no dues or assessments but is charged $50 per visit plus bird costs and is limited to 5 visits per year including as a guest. A Nonresident Member has no turkey, or deer hunting privileges. If a Nonresident member relocates their primary residence to within 150 miles of the Club, such Nonresident Member must apply for reinstatement to the Club for Active Membership. The Nonresident Member will have priority placement on the waiting list and will pay $1000 towards the initiation fee, nonrefundable, as do the current top 10 wait list candidates. Upon acceptance, and an available Active Member opening, such Nonresident Member will become an Active Member, i.e., bypass provisional status. The initiation fee due will be the difference between what they originally paid as initiation fee and the initiation fee in effect at the time of their reapplication.</p>								</div>
